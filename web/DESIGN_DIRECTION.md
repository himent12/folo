# FOLO Studio design direction

Written after round 4 by a design-director pass (Claude Opus 5.5) over every round-4 screenshot and the source.
Round 5 onwards is implemented against this document; per-issue fixes come after the system changes in §5.

## 1. Diagnosis

The screens are competent everywhere and memorable nowhere. Each weak area traces back to the system:

| Area | Local / structural | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Data viz (5.5) | Structural | Four chart implementations (LineChart, Histogram, PrCurves, ConfusionMatrix) with different axis typography; every single-series chart is sky-blue with an area fill, so a run page is six equal-weight charts; hover opens six floating tooltips that repeat the legend values. |
| Typography (6) | Structural | 11 px in 79 places; hard-coded 9/10/15 px; mono leaks into numbers (Playground, PR/histogram/confusion ticks); panel titles 13/500 = body. |
| Colour (6) | Structural | Lime means primary button, running, hero metrics, "best" across incomparable datasets, selected cards, theme previews, sidebar overline. Status hues used as links ("See Health" red, "+1 queued" blue); queued grey on Overview, blue on Runs. |
| Consistency (6) | Structural | No shared table (eight hand-rolled `<table>`s), no chart panel, no page grid. Errors look different per screen. Mobile lists use three card patterns; the top bar shows a title on some screens, only the logo on others. |
| Spacing (6.5) | Mostly structural | Off-grid 10 px (16×), 5, 7, 3 px in components; no column system (8+4, 7+5, thirds), so panel bottoms and inner edges never align. |
| Responsiveness (6.5) | Conceptual | Phone = desktop stacked in one column. The run page on a phone opens with Stop then six charts. |

**The five structural caps**
1. No primitives for the three jobs that make up 80 % of the app: **table, chart panel, page/section grid.** Every screen re-solves them slightly differently.
2. **Colour is decoration, not meaning.** Hue should identify an entity (a run in Compare, a class); right now it is applied by default.
3. **Lime is diluted** (20+ uses), so it no longer reads as "now" or "act".
4. **Type scale too small and too wide**: 7+ sizes per screen, most text at 11–12 px, no panel-title step.
5. **Equal-weight dashboards**: Run, Model, Overview and Compare tile same-weight panels with no single focal object.

## 2. Principles

1. **Density.** List rows 36 px single-line; dense rows (per-class, detections, config, labels) 28 px; two-line rows only on phones (at least 56 px, rule 12). Panel header a fixed 40 px with 16 px side padding; panel body padding 16 (12 dense); gap between panels 16 everywhere. Every panel-like surface (ui/Panel, KPI strips, Health, cards, folds, callouts and their skeletons) draws its outer edge with one alias, `--line-panel` (`base.css`: `line.default` in light, `line.subtle` in dark), never a `--line-*` of its own or a theme override; a strip whose 1 px dividers are a line-coloured background clips it to the padding box (`background-clip: padding-box`), or the translucent edge composites over it and reads darker (`design/panelEdge.test.ts`).
2. **Page grid.** Content max 1200 px (`size.content_max`, the content column without the page's side gutters: list pages, detail pages and forms alike), 12 columns, 16 px gutters. Allowed splits: 12, 8+4, 6+6, 4+4+4. Panels in a row stretch to equal height; panel titles share a baseline; plot left edges align to column edge + 16. A right rail is always 4 columns and sticky (top 64). **A grid of cards never leaves one orphan:** cards side by side (Overview's Datasets and Best models, New run's dataset cards) take as many columns of their minimum width as fit, fewer when that would leave a single card alone on the last row (`ui/balancedGrid`, `use:balanced`: four datasets are 2 × 2, never 3 + 1; `balancedGrid.test.ts`), since a plain `auto-fill` grid cannot know how many cards it holds.
3. **Typographic rhythm.** One job per size, at most six per screen: 20/600 page title · 14/500 panel title · 13/400 body and table cells · 12/400 meta, table headers, legends · 11/400 chart ticks only · 24/600 KPI values. At most one 32/600 hero number per page. No 44 px step, no uppercase overlines in app screens (micro only for key hints). Mono for identifiers, paths, logs, JSON and CLI, never for a number. Helper text 12 px, ≤ 60 characters per line. **Measure:** every helper or explanatory paragraph (Field and Switch hints, Settings notes, Health findings, a callout's Why? fold, an empty state's sentence, a dialog's note) caps its `max-inline-size` at `--measure` (`base.css`, or the `.measure` class), 40ch: a `ch` is Geist's 0, a tabular digit about 1.5 average characters wide, so 40ch is ~60 characters where a "60ch" ran ~90; no component sets a ch width of its own (`ui/copy.test.ts`). **Inside a dialog the measure is the dialog's text column**: `ui/Dialog` sets `--measure` to `--measure-dialog` (60ch), so a note, a hint or a body paragraph runs to the 480 px dialog's padding (440 px) instead of stopping at 40ch with its right third empty. **Prose fills its frame on purpose:** explanatory text in a full-width panel or fold (Engine benchmarks' "How this was measured") is never one 40ch column at the far left of 1,200 px: the setup is a compact facts grid (term, value), and the paragraphs that qualify it sit in columns of at least 36ch (`repeat(auto-fill, minmax(36ch, 1fr))`), each still at the measure, two across where they fit. **Copy is for users:** nothing on screen names a developer document or a source path ("docs/BENCHMARKS.md's figures"); the reference figures are "FOLO's reference benchmarks (RTX 4080 SUPER)" (`referenceSource`), a figure measured on another GPU says so in words ("measured on another GPU (RTX 3060), so not used for this one"), and a wide estimate range says why on its basis line ("Wide range: not run on this GPU yet", `referenceBasis`). A space at the edge of a `{#if}` block is written `{' '}`, since Svelte trims the whitespace a block starts or ends with ("served from(…)"); `ui/copy.test.ts` fails on a doc path in markup or a string and on a bare edge space. **Identifiers vs names:** an architecture (`folo-s`), the checkpoint a run started from (a model cited as a source, `folo-s-coco80`, or a `.folo` file), and a server id (`r_…`, `m_…`) are identifiers and always mono, through one component, `ui/Ident` (a link with `href`), in tables, chips, meta lines, rails and sentences alike; the source scan in `ui/Ident.svelte.test.ts` fails on a bare one. A run's, model's or dataset's own name, where it labels that entity (its row, title, chip, crumb or link), and class names are names: sans. Prose values in an identifier slot ("From scratch", "auto") stay sans, and so do named optimizers ("AdamW", "SGD"): they are names, not identifiers, and in Geist Mono at 13 px AdamW's struck W reads as "₩"; every screen names an optimizer through `optimizerLabel` (`run/resolved`: "Auto (AdamW for short runs, else SGD)", "SGD", "AdamW"). Text that cannot hold markup (a native `<option>`, a palette hint string) never carries an identifier: a select names the entity only, the palette passes it as its own `{ ident }` part (the scan fails on an `arch` in a `label:` / `hint:` template). Commands are code: `folo serve` in a sentence is a mono span, a command to run is one `ui/CommandLine` (or `ui/Remedy`, which holds one), never a `<code>` cut with an ellipsis (the scan fails on a bare `folo <command>` in markup). **The checkpoint a run started from** is named one way everywhere (`ui/Lineage` over `lib/lineage`): the model's name, linked, its server id in the tooltip ("Model m_shapes_n"); a file by its file name, the path in the tooltip. **Group labels** are one style, `.group-label` in `base.css` (12/500 `text.secondary`, sentence case): Run's Config groups (Training, Optimizer, Augmentation), the loss view's Smoothing and Y scale, Settings' benchmark sections, the palette's groups; a component sets their spacing, never their type or ink. **Code in a sentence** (a server's message: "`folo train --amp` asks for them") is `ui/CodeText`: mono, each word of a span one unbreakable run, so `--amp` never splits at its hyphen on a phone. **Meta lines** use one separator, `ui/Meta`: one element per item, a tertiary " · " with 8 px each side, never a dot written by hand and never wide gaps (a callout whose cause is facts, "Completed in 53m 30s · best mAP50-95 0.604 at epoch 150 · saved as a model", passes them as `facts` and the title is the line's first item); a wrapped line never ends (or starts) on a "·", and `line` keeps a desktop table row's items on one line (on phones it wraps like any meta line). **Never ellipsize a fact**: an ellipsis may shorten a free-text name (a run's, a dataset's, a class's) only when the full name is reachable (its title, its own page), never a count, a unit, a status, an identifier or a distinguishing suffix (`shapes-hard-s` beside `shapes-hard-m`); every `ui/Meta` item is a fact, and so is any span marked `data-fact`. A row restructures instead: on phones it wraps whole items (a meta line never cuts its last item), moves a fact to a line of its own (Runs' failure cause over the epoch and dataset; Datasets' image total and class count over the splits; the palette's hint under its name), or drops the least useful part; Compare's phone legend is two columns of compact keys, each the run's whole name on its first line (wrapping at its hyphens, never cut) and its value with the status word on the second ("0.494 · failed"). The shoot's QA reports any truncated box that holds a fact (§6). **Tertiary is for skimming**: `text.tertiary` is for labels and metadata people skim (a stat's label, column heads, keys, dates, separators); figures people read are `text.secondary`: KPI captions ("best 0.604 · ep 150", "warmup 360 steps · linear", "at conf 0.39 (best F1)"), a rail's measured conditions, a benchmark row's sub-line, a ranked list's counts. **A note after a value** ("0.000833 (requested 0.01)", "AdamW (auto)", "360 steps (3 epochs)") is `ui/Note` wherever it appears (Run's Config, Compare's table): a real, breakable space, then the note in 12 px tertiary that never breaks inside (`ui/Note.svelte.test.ts` fails on a hand-rolled note span). **Conditions are meta lines**: a value's conditions (Model's measured and reference latency) are a `ui/Meta` line each, so "RTX 4080 SUPER" never breaks inside, never a sentence in a cell with `overflow-wrap: anywhere`; an identifier in a chip (Compare's "From `fasteners-n-best`") wraps after a hyphen onto a second line rather than lose its end. **Wrapping**: prose wraps `text-wrap: pretty` (set once on `html`), so hints, help, dialogs and banners never end on an orphan; a key group never breaks (`ui/Kbd`), and punctuation after it rides along in `.nowrap` ("Ctrl V."). **Value rows** (KPI tabs, stats) are a fixed line box: a unit or exponent is `line-height: 1` and never deepens the line, so values with and without units share a baseline in every tier.
4. **Chart frame.** Header (40 px): title 14/500, unit or qualifier tertiary, controls right (at most one segmented group). Legend row (24 px) directly below, starting at the plot's left edge. **Legend values are the readout**: last value at rest, cursor value on hover; one shared vertical cursor across synced charts; no floating tooltip. Plot: horizontal gridlines only (3–5), baseline axis, y ticks right-aligned in a 32 px gutter, 11 px Geist ticks; the x-axis name goes into the header ("by epoch", "by progress, % of planned epochs", "precision by recall", "boxes by size, % of the image side"), never a caption under the x axis or over the y ticks; the one y caption is the zoom note below. **The exception is a chart of one quantity against another on the same 0–1 scale** (PR curves: precision against recall), where 0–1 ticks on both axes cannot say which is which: it names both axes at the axes, `xLabel="Recall"` under the x ticks and `yLabel="Precision"` over the y ticks (`model/PrCurves`), and the room for both is kept at every width, phones included (a phone's plot keeps its 190 px and the chart grows by the captions). **One tick rule at every width**: a fitted domain's step and decimals come from the domain alone (`lib/charts/domain`, `Fit.decimals`), never from the plot's height, so 0–0.86 reads 0.0 · 0.2 · … · 0.8 on a desktop; a plot too short for every tick at 32 px keeps every second one of the same step with the same decimals (0.0 · 0.4 · 0.8 on a phone), never a different step (`domain.test.ts`). A chart panel never contains a second legend, a paragraph, an overline, a floating "best" label (use the ink best ring and the value in the header's qualifier), or more than one area fill. **A y domain fits its data** (`lib/charts/domain`): a 0–1 metric runs from 0 to its maximum plus 5 % headroom (`unitTop`, so the data reaches at least 80 % of the plot), with 3–5 nice gridlines under that top, never a fixed 0–1 with two-thirds of the plot empty; under 0.25 the axis is zoomed and says so in its y caption ("Zoomed in · full scale 0–1", `UNIT_ZOOM_NOTE`), so a run stuck near 0 still reads as low. Every chart of mAP, precision or recall that is not 0–1 by nature (PR curves are) takes `unitDomain` (`domain.test.ts`). A comparison's plot is at least 360 px tall from 768 px up. **Direct labels** (`endLabels`) are anchored to their line's end marker: beside it when that spot is clear, otherwise in the column right of the plot (the gutter) tied to the marker by a leader; inside the plot a label sits beside its marker, never further out on a leader (round 24: a label floating mid-plot on a leader), save the last resort below, never closer than 12 px to another label (`LABEL_GAP`, enforced in `placeEndLabels` whatever a caller asks, measured between the boxes the browser draws: the label's measured width by its 16 px text line, `LABEL_H`; `endLabels.test.ts`); their width is measured, not estimated. **Other runs' lines are obstacles**: a label keeps 6 px clear of every line but its own (`CLEAR`; 2 px off another run's line reads as sitting on it), and a leader crosses no other line wherever a spot beside the marker or in the gutter allows it (`endLabels.test.ts`). **Every line end gets its label** (from 560 px up): a line that ends among the others with no spot beside it and none in the gutter within 64 px (round 25: Compare's failed shapes-hard-m, its only cue a red ×) takes, as the last resort, the clear spot in the plot or the gutter whose leader crosses the fewest other lines, nearest its end (`endLabels` `lastResort`); only a plot with no clear box at all leaves a label out. **One rule for direct labels at every chart:** a plot under 560 px (`END_LABELS_MIN`, every phone) draws none, short names such as Loss's "box", "cls", "dfl" included, and gives that width to the data; its legend carries each line's name, value and how it ended (`LineChart.svelte.test.ts`). A chart with direct labels leaves them a row inside the plot: its 0–1 top is fitted to the highest end raised by a label row (`withLabelRow`, 24 px), so a label set above its line never sits on the top gridline. **One focal-chart vocabulary:** a run's focal chart (Run's metric chart, Overview's live card) marks the best epoch with a hollow ink ring (`text.primary`, 1.5 px, the "○ best" glyph of its header qualifier, "○ best at ep 36"; pulsed once on a new best) and the line's end with the status end mark (`end`: a filled lime dot with the heartbeat while live, the running mark; a square stopped; a cross failed; a plain dot in the line's ink completed). **Each mark has one shape for one meaning, whatever sits next to it** (`charts/endMarks`): the ring is always the best and always ink, on a live, completed, stopped or failed run; the filled lime dot is always the latest value of a live run; so best at epoch 36 and live at 37 read as a ring and a dot, never two identical marks ("oo", round 25). Only when the best is the line's end, or so close that the two would overlap (`MERGE_PX`, under 2 px clear: a phone's epochs 36 and 37), is the ring drawn around the end mark as one mark; the status mark is always drawn, a failed run's cross in the danger ink, never covered. Every chart that draws end marks (Run, Overview's live card, Compare) goes through `LineChart`'s `endDots`, so the rule holds everywhere (`endMarks.test.ts`, `LineChart.svelte.test.ts`). **The one brand moment on a chart:** when a run completes while its page is open, lime viewfinder corners close in on its best ring over 200 ms, hold and fade (`Marker.focus`, `charts/bestPulse` `CompleteFocus`; none under reduced motion).
5. **Colour by entity.** Hue identifies an entity: a run in Compare, a class in evaluation. A single run's metrics are drawn in ink: the primary metric `text.primary` 1.5 px, secondary metrics `text.tertiary` with dash patterns. Loss: total primary; box / cls / dfl are three quantities, so they take the hues design/README.md gives them in both apps (box amber, cls violet dashed, dfl teal dotted). Histograms and timing bars neutral ink. Area fill only on the page's single focal series, at 8 %. A series' **fills** (legend swatches, run chips, table keys) use `chart.fill`, the exact palette hue in both themes with the `chart.swatch_edge` hairline, as class swatches do; only its 1–2 px line takes the light theme's darker ink (`chart.series`), so a light amber run reads amber, never brown. **Series hue in text** (direct end labels, the Playground's JSON tokens, any 12–13 px text in a series' hue) is `chart.text` (`--chart-text-N`, `charts/ink` `seriesText`), which `design/contrast.mjs` holds to 4.5:1 on every surface in both themes: chart colours are for marks and swatches, never small text (`charts/ink.test.ts` fails on `color: var(--chart-N)`). **Patterned series** (dashed, dotted) draw in that darker text ink and, in light, at 2 px, so a dotted fourth run clears 3:1 in practice and not only per pixel.
6. **Lime means "now" or "the one action".** Allowed: one primary button per viewport, focus ring, running dot and live progress (a live line's end dot), selected detection, the completion focus on a best ring (§2.4). The best-epoch ring itself is ink. **Lime is never a control's state or a failed run:** a switch that is on is the ink track (`text.primary`) with an inverse knob (`text.inverse`), as checkboxes are ink (`ui/Switch`), so New run's Mixed precision never competes with Start training; the best ring is ink on every run (§2.4). **A status shows through its icon and its callout's tint, never its words:** a callout's title and its links ("Ran out of GPU memory", "Too short to learn:", "Why?", an error state's "Prediction failed") are ink, the icon takes the status hue, and a callout that asks for action (danger, warning) is a banner with its status's muted tint (`run/Callout`); this also keeps amber text, weak in light, off the screen (`design/statusHue.test.ts`). A field's own error message under it stays in the danger ink (§2.11). Never a selection (a selected row, option, chip or cell is the ink bar and `bg.active`, §2.7), metric text, meters, nav icons (the current section is `text.primary` on `bg.active` in the sidebar, the icon rail, the tab bar and the palette; `shell/navAccent.test.ts`), selected cards (use `line.strong` + `bg.active`, neutral), illustrations, previews. Status colours: dot + word in tables, tinted fills only in banners; the tinted status pill (`ui/StatusBadge` without `plain`) is the title form, beside the name of the one run a screen is about (the run page's header, Overview's live card), never in a table, list or legend (`ui/statusPill.test.ts`); **a selection adds a mark and takes no fact away**: a chosen card keeps everything it showed (New run's dataset cards keep their format; the check goes before the name); queued and stopped neutral; where a status has no room for its word (the collapsed sidebar's connection) it is a glyph with the word as its accessible name and tooltip ("Server offline"), never a lone coloured dot. Once a list's selection enables its action (Runs' Compare, at two runs), that action is the one primary and the page's create action steps back to secondary; until then (one run selected, Compare disabled with its reason) the create action stays primary, so a state never has no primary. Links are `text.primary` (underline on hover), never a status hue; a toast's action ("Open run") is a small secondary button in ink, never a lime link. **Status hue belongs to the mark, not the metric:** a KPI or stat value stays in ink and its status is an icon in the hue beside its words in the caption ("3 · ⚠ not trained on"; `ui/Stat` has no tone). The same for every value: a table's best value is ink at 600 with a tertiary "best" word (Compare's side by side, the Models list), never lime text or an accent chip; a value with a problem (New run's Summary showing a device the server cannot find) stays in ink with the danger icon before it, and the row's accessible name says "needs attention"; an inline action ("Lower the threshold") is a link in ink. `design/statusHue.test.ts` fails on a status or accent hue on a value slot (`.num`, `.winner`, `.rv-v`, `-v`), on an accent tint outside `ui/Badge` and `ui/StatusBadge`, and on a lime link. **One condition, one severity:** a forecast over the GPU's memory is danger on its meter and on the line under it (icon + words), within 10 % of it warning, and the figure itself stays in ink. **Demo data is a mode, not a degraded state:** a neutral flask glyph in the sidebar and Settings › Connection, never the warning dot (`design/statusHue.test.ts`).
7. **Tables.** One system: the global `.table` class (`lib/design/base.css`) is the single source, so every table looks the same without local styles. Header 32 px, 12/500 tertiary, sentence case; rows 36 px single-line, `.dense` 28, `.tall` 48 (a name over one meta line); the first cell (or `.lead` when a checkbox or index comes first) is 13/500 primary and its second line is `.sub` (12/400 tertiary); everything else 13/400 (secondary for specs and reference columns); numbers right-aligned (`.r`) and tabular with the unit in the header, not the cell; at most one inline bar column (48 px, neutral; 0–1 while any row reaches 0.5, else scaled to just over the column's best with `unitTop`, and the panel's meta line says so, "bars 0–0.044", so an AP of 0.04 is never a dot); status as dot + word, not a pill. Rows that open something are `.link` (hover fill); a hover fill exists only under `@media (hover: hover)`, is cleared on `pointerleave` (never set by a tap or a click's focus) and never replaces a selected row's fill, so a list shows one highlight per meaning; **selection is ink, never lime**: `.on` / `aria-selected` is `bg.active` and a 2 px `text.primary` bar on the leading edge (`--select-bar` in `base.css`) in every list and option set: tables, Runs' phone rows, New run's Model size rows, the inspector's label rows, Model's traced classes, Compare's pinned run chip, and a pinned confusion-matrix cell's 2 px stroke. Lime is "now" or the one action (§2.6), so it never marks what is chosen; the one exception is a selected detection on an image (`lib/detect/DetectionView`, in the inspector's and the Playground's canvas), whose lime must read on any photo (`design/statusHue.test.ts` and `shell/navAccent.test.ts` fail on an accent in any selection rule or an accent leading bar). A keyboard-focused row (rowNav) draws the focus ring as a closed rectangle 4 px inside the row (measured: the 2 px ring at offset −6 px, 2 px clear of the 2 px bar), so ring and bar stay two shapes in two inks; no table sets a ring of its own; row checkboxes keep their column's width but show only on the hovered or keyboard-focused row (so X is discoverable), on selected rows, and on every row once any is selected; on touch they always show, 20 px in a ≥ 44 × 44 px cell. A checked box is neutral (`text.primary` fill, inverted check), like the row's ink bar: a checked row carries no lime. **Responsive columns** are one global rule in `base.css`: one class per layout tier (18), `.hide-lg` (< 1280), `.hide-md` (< 1024), `.hide-sm` (< 768), go on the `<th>` (a `SortHeader` takes it as `class`) and on every `<td>` of the column, skeleton rows included; a component never defines its own, since a scoped rule cannot reach a header rendered by `SortHeader` (`listTables.svelte.test.ts` checks header and body cells per row at each width). A sortable header is `ui/SortHeader` (a button with the direction mark, `aria-sort` on the sorted column only, the mark's slot kept so labels never shift; number columns put the mark before the label). **One geometry in every state:** every column of a list table but its flexible one(s) has a fixed width set once, on the header row that loading and loaded share (a `width` on `<th>` or `SortHeader`; widths on the header rather than a `<colgroup>`, since a `<col>` hidden by `.hide-*` corrupts the column map), so a column sits at the same x in every state (`listTables.svelte.test.ts`); a skeleton bar in a `.r` cell sits on the right edge (`base.css`); a row-checkbox column is a real 32 px column that is always there, so showing the boxes never moves the name; a column that holds a strip fills it from a minimum (`minmax(160px, 1fr)`, Datasets' class balance); a table of a few rows across a wide panel (Model's per class, Datasets) takes bounded widths (96 px numbers, a 224 px name, Datasets' path 280 px and class balance 184 px, its 160 px strip plus padding; Compare's side by side with two or three runs from 1024 px up, 224 px run columns) and leaves the rest to an empty last column (`.rest`, hidden with the columns it pairs with), never figures strewn across 1,000 px or a path cell holding 500 px of nothing. A figure whose source differs by row says it in its own cell, one tertiary word ("5.90 ms measured", "7.87 ms reference": New run's sizes), so the caption under the table stays one line. **With no rows** (empty, no match, error) the frame drops its column heads and holds the `TableState` alone, never a row of nine to eleven heads over a one-line message; **the list's toolbar is always there** (filter and field, at their loaded place), disabled with no counts over an empty or failed list, so the frame starts at the same y in loading, loaded, empty and error (`listTables.svelte.test.ts`). List tables (Runs, Models, Datasets) take `use:rowNav` and `data-row`: one roving tab stop, J / K and ↓ / ↑ move it, Enter opens, X selects for Compare on Runs, J / K enter the list when nothing has focus. The defaults are wrapped in `:where()`, so a table's own classes always win; a table that needs a local rule for the header, the first column, hover or selection is a bug in the system, not in the table.
8. **States** render inside the frame that would hold the data. One height policy for every list (Runs, Models, Datasets, Overview's Recent runs): the toolbar above stays (disabled while there is nothing to filter) and the frame, without its column heads, is a compact block, the message and its actions with the panel's 16 px padding, never held at the loaded rows' or the skeleton's height. On phones (no header row) the card is the content plus 24 px (12 above, 12 below). In a table that is `ui/TableState`; panels side by side in a row stretch to equal height, so a short state in one never leaves the other hanging. Loading is the exception: the skeleton mirrors the loaded rows. Empty: 16 px icon (no frame), one sentence, one button (secondary unless the page has no other primary). Error: same placement, `danger.text` title, a cause sentence, a mono request line, Retry. **A page's structure never repeats itself:** when nothing has loaded and every request failed, a dashboard (Overview) is one recovery block (the cause, Retry and Connection settings) in the frame its focal object would fill (the live card's "Training" panel), never four panels each saying the server has not answered; when the server stops answering after data arrived, the page keeps what it has under one stale-data notice ("Server not answering · Showing what loaded 2m ago", Retry; `Resource.stale`), never a panel-by-panel error. A list page with one focal table still states its error in that table's frame. A page whose header holds a create action (Runs' New run, Datasets' Add dataset) calls `ownCreateAction()` (`shell/crumbs`) and the top bar's New run steps aside while it is mounted, so one corner never holds two create buttons. Offline, the actions that need the server (New run, Add dataset, the top bar's New run) are disabled, not lime, with the reason "Server offline" as a tooltip and as their accessible description (`ui/Gate`); the same wrapper gives every disabled control its reason ("Select 2–4 runs to compare"). **A prerequisite is visible, never hover-only:** a tooltip never shows to touch, nor to a keyboard (a disabled button takes no focus), so a reason the person can act on is also a line of words beside or under the control (`data-gate-why`): Runs' "Select one more run to compare · 2–4 runs", which follows the selection (0, 1, 2–3 and 4 selected) from 1024 px beside Compare, on tablets at the end of the toolbar's filter row (the header keeps one action row: Compare, New run) and on phones under the list's Cancel and Compare; Add dataset's "Enter a folder path" at the footer's start. Only "Server offline" is not repeated, since the page says it (`ui/gate.test.ts`). **A field in error offers its fix where one can be derived:** Field's `fix` is one click after the message ("Letters, digits … only. Try my-dataset"). Loading: skeletons mirror the final geometry exactly; charts show the axis frame without a line; an image's skeleton (`Skeleton image`) is a raised fill with `line.strong` viewfinder corners (in light, a grey well a step below the app background, since a near-white raised fill reads as a blank page), so it never reads as an empty photo. ## 6. Checks in the shoot

`npm run shoot` runs three checks on every shot after its setup and lists what they find together, printed last, boxed,
and saved to `qa.txt` beside the shots (`qa-<filter>.txt` with `--only`). The list never fails the shoot; review it with
the screenshots. It must be empty before a round goes to review.

**`npm run qa`** (`shoot --qa`, run by CI's `web` job after the build) runs the same checks without the shots and
exits 1 if they find anything, printing the list. It covers where phone layouts break: every scene at the `tablet` and
`mobile` sizes it lists, in dark (or the one theme a scene is made for), and again in light wherever the page shows a
phone section picker (Run's and Model's sections, chosen by the scene or by default). Each state gets the scene's own
setup; the page clock is paused at the fixed time before the page loads, so a state never depends on how busy the
machine is (in the shoot it keeps running in real time). Several states run at once (`FOLO_QA_JOBS`, default up to 8):
about 1 to 2 minutes for some 150 states on the dev PC, a little over 1 minute on a boat.dev runner. It uses the
installed Chrome, else Playwright's Chromium (`npx playwright-core install --only-shell chromium`;
`FOLO_SHOOT_BROWSER=chromium` forces it, `chrome` refuses the fallback). Desktop and laptop are left to the shoot.

**Blank sections.** A selected control whose content renders nothing is the bug round 19 shipped (the phone rule that
hid every `.rail` left Model's Details, Run's Classes and a queued run's Config blank behind a selected picker):
- every selected tab (`[role=tab][aria-selected=true]`) and every checked radio with `aria-controls`: the element it
  controls must exist, be visible (not `display: none`, itself or through an ancestor) and be taller than 40 px;
- every checked radio of a section picker without `aria-controls` (a Segmented labelled "Show", or one in
  `.phone-pick`): the content under the picker in `main` must reach 40 px, and start 12 px under the picker (sections
  share one geometry: a hidden section leaves no grid track or gap behind);
- `main`: the content under the page header (`.page > header`, or main's top when the header takes no room) must reach
  80 px.

Content is what a reader sees: text, images, canvases, SVG, form controls, and boxes with a fill or an edge (panel
frames, skeleton blocks); fixed bars (the tab bar, docks) don't count. A section picker's sections should therefore keep
their content in the page's own region, not in a column a breakpoint can hide. The check was proven against the round-19
rule: put back `.app :global(.rail) { display: none }` in `App.svelte` and `npm run qa` fails on `model-detail-details`,
`run-live-phone-classes`, `run-queued` and `model-o2o` at mobile, in both themes.

**Cut facts.** Any box cut with an ellipsis (`text-overflow: ellipsis`, its content wider than it) that holds a fact: a
`ui/Meta` line or a span marked `data-fact` (§2.3); and any element in one whose content runs past its box without an
ellipsis (clipped or covered: round 25's "Completed in 53m 3" under the callout's button). A `clip-path` counts as
hiding only a box it hides (visually hidden text), never `ui/Meta`'s edge clip. A truncated free-text name alone is not listed. The check found the

**Horizontal overflow.** The page wider than the viewport, and any element whose content is wider than its box
(`scrollWidth > clientWidth + 1`, innermost box, naming its widest child). It skips hidden content, ellipsis truncation, negative-margin hit areas, positioned decorations and deliberate scrollers
(`data-scrolls`, or an `overflow-x: auto` box with a fade mask). A plain scrollbar is not a deliberate scroller: a list
table that needs one at a width it was designed for is the bug the check exists to catch. All three share the list above.
