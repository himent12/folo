# Code of conduct

FOLO follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Criticise code, measurements and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, or publishing of anyone's private information.
- Keep discussion about the work: a claim comes with what was run and what was measured.

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and anywhere someone represents the
project.

To report behaviour that breaks it, contact the maintainer, [@himent12](https://github.com/himent12), privately, or
use GitHub's own [report tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
on the comment or profile. Reports are handled in confidence. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues
and pull requests that break this code, and may block a contributor who keeps breaking it.
